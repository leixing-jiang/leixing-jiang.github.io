Working Papers

Does Democratizing Search for Relevant Patent Information Facilitate Knowledge Recombination?

Job Market Paper

Abstract

This study examines whether reducing search costs for relevant patent information increases corporate innovation. I exploit the 2013 adoption of the Cooperative Patent Classification by U.S. and European patent authorities as a natural experiment. Following its adoption, public U.S. firms with greater exposure to non-English European patents experience larger increases in innovation activity relative to less exposed firms. The gain is driven by improved efficiency in knowledge recombination: firms generate more output per unit of cumulative R&D, rely on more related prior knowledge, and increase their use of European patents. Overall, the findings highlight the importance of searchability in patent disclosure in facilitating innovation.
